How to Sell Your House to Us?

It is as simple as it looks

Submit Property Info

Tell us about your property using our simple form.

After we receive the initial information through our web form, Geraldo or another member of our team will follow up with any additional questions needed to move forward.

Receive a Cash Offer

We'll review your property and send you a written offer.

Once we have all the necessary information, we will review comparable properties that have recently sold in the area to determine a fair market value and present you with an offer.

Close on Your Schedule

Pick the closing date that works best for you.

If you decide to accept our offer, we'll work with you to choose a closing date that fits your schedule and make sure you get paid on time.

No matter the condition of your property or your circumstances, we'll work together to find a solution that works for you

Intro

We are a Family Real Estate Investment Company focused on finding new, used, and distressed property opportunities. Proudly serving communities across Michigan and Ohio. Our goal is to continue growing our portfolio by purchasing more properties across Grand Rapids and Detroit this time.

We buy homes in cash, with no realtor commissions, no inspection requirements, and no hidden fees.

Our process is simple, fast, and hassle-free, allowing you as a homeowner to sell quickly and close immediately with confidence.

The Work we do

As investors, we work closely with homeowners, investors, and realtors to improve properties, strengthen neighborhoods, and create reliable real estate opportunities.

Once we acquire properties, we carefully manage and improve them based on current market needs and long-term growth potential, helping contribute to stronger neighborhoods and better communities for every citizen of America.
